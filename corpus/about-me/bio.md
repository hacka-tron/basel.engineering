# About Basel

## Who I am

Basel Abdel-Rahman is a backend and platform engineer with 6+ years building large-scale distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and developer tooling at Google and Microsoft. His work spans designing resilient backend services, infrastructure-as-code, safe deployment and release engineering, and automated validation that gates production releases at scale. He's taken mission-critical systems from design through global production rollout, with a consistent focus on reliability, observability, security, and developer productivity.

## Current role

Basel is currently a Software Engineer at Microsoft (July 2024–present) on the core Azure team, working on a service that catalogs over 35 million virtual containers and physical assets globally. His work there covers reliability engineering (distributed rate-limiting, deny-list controls), infrastructure-as-code (ARM templates, Ev2 safe deployment for Service Fabric clusters), release engineering (service decoupling, CI/CD standardization), and security hardening. See `microsoft.md` for the full detail.

## Background

Before Microsoft, Basel spent about 4 years (Sep 2019–July 2023) at Google, working across Fitbit's DevEx team and YouTube's Living Room team on test automation, release-gating infrastructure, and cross-platform device testing. See `google.md` for detail. Between Google and Microsoft, he spent roughly a year (July 2023–July 2024) traveling internationally and upskilling in software architecture and distributed systems ahead of pursuing a more production-focused engineering role.

He holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science from The Ohio State University (Columbus, OH), 2015–2019.
